Flow (step by step)

  1. The alarm clock rings: every 5 minutes the app checks for alerts, every morning (09:00 IST) it searches Google, every hour Gemini checks the news, and at 00:05 the day resets.
  2. When Karan opens the app, it also checks straight away (never more than once every 2 minutes).
  3. The app reads its database: Karan's 60 artists, the city rules, the real shows and the daily sample drops.
  4. It asks Google through SerpApi which concerts are coming to Bengaluru, Mumbai and Chennai (SearchApi.io takes over automatically if a call fails).
  5. Every hour, Gemini (with Google Search) looks for brand-new announcements, 3 artists per run.
  6. The app remembers who Karan loves most, which cities matter, what he was already told and what he tapped.
  7. The Detector spots new shows and festival lineups; the Reminder spots early-birds about to open or close, a show next week, a show tonight; one nudge if a critical alert is ignored for 6 hours.
  8. Gemini matches each concert to the right artist, writes a fun message and gives a "worth the trip?" verdict for Mumbai/Chennai Must-see shows.
  9. Everything is saved with a unique key, so the same email is never sent twice; failed sends are retried up to 5 times.
  10. Karan gets an email for every new show; reminder emails come only for shows he tapped "Remind me" or "Going", so his inbox never feels like spam.
  11. Karan's screen shows what to grab now, every upcoming show by city, a 6-month calendar, his plans, and proof of every Google search.
  12. The owl is just for fun: it flies up and down and says when the app last checked.
  13. Karan taps "Got early-bird", "Going", "Remind me" or "Skip" (with a confetti burst), and the app reminds him only about what he cares about.
